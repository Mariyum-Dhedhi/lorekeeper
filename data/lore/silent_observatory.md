# The Silent Observatory

## Construction
The Silent Observatory stands on the highest peak of Duskholm. It was built in 780 TR by the astronomer Pell Grimwald to track the stars and the migration routes of the sky-whales. Its great lens, a single flawless disc of driftglass called the Eye of Grimwald, was the largest piece of driftglass ever shaped by the Glasswrights.

## Purpose
For nearly four centuries the observatory's scholars predicted storms, charted the Great Passage, and sent their findings by signal lantern to Halcyon Reach. Its records were the main reason Veyrans could predict the timing of the Great Passage so precisely.

## The Silence
In 1150 TR the observatory's signal lantern went dark. A Tidewarden ship sent to investigate found the doors barred from the inside and the Eye of Grimwald cracked from edge to edge. All nine observers had vanished. No bodies were ever found, and the cause of the disappearance remains unknown.

## The Seal
By decree of the Tidecouncil, the Silent Observatory was sealed in 1150 TR, and no one has been permitted to enter it since. Duskholm shepherds claim that on clear nights a faint humming can be heard from the peak.
