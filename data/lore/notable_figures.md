# Notable Figures of Veyra

## Captain Juna Ferrow (1001 to 1068 TR)
Juna Ferrow was Veyra's most celebrated explorer. Sponsored by the Cartographers' Guild, she sailed her ship, the Patient Gull, to the far western limits of the Glass Sea and in 1032 TR mapped the Edge of Mist, the wall of fog beyond which no ship has ever returned. She retired to Saltmere and died there peacefully in 1068 TR.

## Pell Grimwald
Pell Grimwald was an astronomer from Duskholm who built the Silent Observatory in 780 TR. He designed the Eye of Grimwald, its great driftglass lens, and was the first to calculate that the Great Passage repeats every eleven years.

## Oriel Vantis
Oriel Vantis is the current Guildmaster of the Cartographers' Guild. He is said never to have lost his way, even in the thornwood forests of Mirethorn.

## Sabine Korr
Sabine Korr is the current Guildmistress of the Glasswrights' Guild of Kessa Vel. She is famous for shaping a driftglass rose so thin that it rings like a bell when touched.

## Admiral Hesk Marrow
Hesk Marrow commands the Tidewardens from Saltmere. He is one of the few people in Veyra licensed to practice Stormweaving, and the glassing on his left hand has reached the wrist.

## Amaranth Sel
Amaranth Sel is the First Voice of the Hollow Choir. She is said to be able to sing a sky-whale to sleep.
