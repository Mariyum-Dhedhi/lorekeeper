# The Vael (Sky-Whales)

## Description
The Vael, commonly called sky-whales, are enormous creatures that drift through the skies above the Glass Sea. They have no wings; scholars believe they float on pockets of lighter-than-air gas within their bodies. The Vael feed on stormlight, the crackling energy of thunderstorms, and herds of them are often seen trailing behind storm fronts.

## The Great Passage
Every eleven years, the Vael undertake the Great Passage, a migration from the far north, where they first appear above Duskholm, south across the entire archipelago. The passage lasts about a month. The most recent Great Passage took place in 1187 TR, and the next is expected in 1198 TR.

## Old Grandmother
The largest Vael ever recorded is known as Old Grandmother. She was first sighted in 412 TR and was measured at 340 meters from nose to tail. She has been seen on nearly every Great Passage since, which makes her at least several centuries old.

## Driftglass
As they travel, the Vael shed translucent scales that drift down onto the islands and the sea. This material is called driftglass. Freshly fallen driftglass glows faintly for years. The Glasswrights of Kessa Vel shape it into lenses, lamps, and the tether stones that tidecrafters need.

## Danger
Although the Vael are not hostile, a sky-whale that descends too low can flatten a village simply by resting. The Lanterns of Calling and the songs of the Hollow Choir keep them at a safe height.
