# The Seven Islands

## Ostrel
Ostrel is the political heart of Veyra and the site of the capital, Halcyon Reach. Its white cliffs are crowned with terraced gardens. The Lamplighters' Guild keeps its headquarters on Ostrel, beside the Regent's Lantern.

## Mirethorn
Mirethorn is the greenest of the islands, famous for its ancient thornwood forests and for its cartographers. The Cartographers' Guild was founded in Mirethorn's forest town of Quillhollow, and the island's mapmakers are said to be able to chart a coastline from a single glance.

## Kessa Vel
Kessa Vel is a volcanic island whose black sand beaches glitter with obsidian. Its forges and furnaces never cool, and it is the home of the Glasswrights' Guild. The island's volcano, Mount Cindrel, last erupted in 1102 TR.

## Duskholm
Duskholm is the northernmost island, a land of ice fields, long twilights, and frost-covered lichen. The abandoned Silent Observatory stands on its highest peak. The sky-whales begin their Great Passage over Duskholm's northern shores.

## Brineward
Brineward is a broad, low island whose economy depends on fishing. Its harbor city, Saltmere, is the largest port in Veyra. The Tidewardens, Veyra's sea guard, are based in Saltmere.

## Thal's Anvil
Thal's Anvil is a rugged, iron-rich island of mines and smithies. Its smiths forge the anchors, chains, and bells used across the archipelago. It has no Great Guild of its own, a fact its people mention often and with feeling.

## Ilvane
Ilvane is the smallest of the seven islands, and the most sacred. It is the home of the Hollow Choir, whose singers live in caves carved so that the wind itself becomes music. Visitors to Ilvane must remain silent from dusk until dawn.
