# Trade and Economy

## Currency
The currency of Veyra is the tidemark, a silver coin stamped with a lantern. One tidemark is worth twenty pearls, the small copper coins used for everyday purchases. Coins are minted only in Halcyon Reach.

## Exports
Veyra's three great exports are driftglass goods from the furnaces of Kessa Vel, sea charts from the Cartographers' Guild of Mirethorn, and salted emberfish from Brineward.

## The Driftglass Trade
Since the Treaty of Lanterns in 911 TR, the Tidecouncil sets the price of driftglass and licenses everyone who gathers it. Unlicensed driftglass gathering is punished by exile to Duskholm.

## Foreign Trade
Veyra's only regular foreign trading partner is the Coral Dominion, a distant realm far to the south of the Glass Sea. Coral Dominion ships arrive in Saltmere twice a year, bringing spices, dyes, and coral, and leaving with driftglass and charts.
