# The Great Guilds of Veyra

## Overview
Five Great Guilds hold charters from the Tidecouncil. Each guild is tied to a home island, and each has a symbol stitched onto the cloaks of its members.

## The Cartographers' Guild
Based on Mirethorn, the Cartographers' Guild maps the coasts, currents, and sky-routes of Veyra. Its symbol is a silver compass. The current Guildmaster is Oriel Vantis. The guild sponsors exploration voyages to the edges of the known sea.

## The Glasswrights' Guild
Based on Kessa Vel, the Glasswrights' Guild works driftglass, the luminous material shed by the sky-whales, into lenses, lamps, and tether stones. Its symbol is a red flame inside a glass sphere. The current Guildmistress is Sabine Korr.

## The Tidewardens
Based in Saltmere on Brineward, the Tidewardens are the sea guard of Veyra. They patrol the Glass Sea, rescue sailors, and are the only body licensed to practice Stormweaving. Their symbol is a crossed anchor and oar. They are commanded by Admiral Hesk Marrow.

## The Lamplighters' Guild
Based on Ostrel, the Lamplighters' Guild tends the forty-nine Lanterns of Calling that stand on the shores of every island. The lanterns keep the sky-whales from descending onto inhabited land. Every year on Stillday, the Lamplighters lead the ceremony of Lantern Night. Their symbol is a golden wick.

## The Hollow Choir
Based on Ilvane, the Hollow Choir is both a guild and a religious order. Its singers practice Echosong, singing to the sky-whales during the Great Passage to guide them safely past the islands. The Choir is led by a singer titled the First Voice; the current First Voice is Amaranth Sel. Its symbol is an open mouth with a spiral at its center.
