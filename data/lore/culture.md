# Culture and Customs of Veyra

## Greetings
The traditional Veyran greeting is "Fair tides," to which the polite reply is "And calm skies." Sailors consider it bad luck to begin a voyage without exchanging these words with someone on the dock.

## Food and Drink
Emberfish stew is the signature dish of Kessa Vel: a fiery red stew of emberfish cooked in a clay pot buried in volcanic sand. Saltbread, a dense loaf baked with seawater, is eaten on every island. Stormtea is a bitter, warming drink brewed from frostlichen, a pale lichen that grows only on Duskholm. Tidecrafters drink it in large quantities to fight the chill.

## Music
Music is central to Veyran life. Fishing crews sing work songs to keep their rhythm. Out of respect for the Hollow Choir, it is considered rude to sing loudly on Ilvane.

## Superstitions
Breaking a piece of driftglass on purpose is said to bring eleven years of bad luck, one for each year between Great Passages. Whistling at sea is believed to summon storms, a belief that has grown stronger since the Stormwar.
