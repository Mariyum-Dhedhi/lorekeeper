# A History of Veyra

## The Sundering (0 TR)
The continent of Aumar broke apart and sank beneath the waves. Only its highest mountains remained above water, becoming the seven islands of Veyra. Survivors began the count of years now called the Tideborn Reckoning.

## The Scattered Centuries (0 to 603 TR)
For six centuries the islands were ruled separately by rival island lords. In 412 TR the great sky-whale Old Grandmother was first sighted, and her appearances were later used to date events of this era.

## The Treaty of Saltmere (603 TR)
In 603 TR the lords of all seven islands met in the port of Saltmere and signed the Treaty of Saltmere, which unified the islands under a single Tidecouncil. Ysolde Varn was chosen as the first Lantern Regent.

## The Stormwar (902 to 911 TR)
The Stormwar was fought between Kessa Vel and Brineward over the right to gather driftglass from the waters between them. Both sides used Stormweaving, and the storms they summoned sank hundreds of ships and drowned the coastal villages of both islands. The war ended in 911 TR with the Treaty of Lanterns, signed under the Regent's Lantern in Halcyon Reach. The treaty banned Stormweaving and placed the driftglass trade under the control of the Tidecouncil.

## The Silence (1150 TR)
In 1150 TR, all nine observers of the Silent Observatory on Duskholm vanished in a single night. The Tidecouncil sealed the observatory, and it has remained closed ever since.

## Recent Times
The most recent Great Passage took place in 1187 TR. Lantern Regent Maelis Thorne has led the Tidecouncil since 1179 TR.
