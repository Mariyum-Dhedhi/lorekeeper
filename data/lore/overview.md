# Veyra: An Overview

## The Archipelago
Veyra is an archipelago of seven major islands scattered across the Glass Sea, a calm and unusually clear body of water that reflects the sky so perfectly that sailors say it is hard to tell where the sea ends and the clouds begin. Roughly two million people live across the islands. Countless smaller islets and sandbars dot the sea between the seven, but none of them are permanently settled.

## The Capital
The capital of Veyra is Halcyon Reach, a terraced city built into the cliffs of the island of Ostrel. Halcyon Reach is home to the Tidecouncil chamber, the Great Library, and the Regent's Lantern, the tallest of all the Lanterns of Calling.

## Government at a Glance
Veyra is governed by the Tidecouncil, an assembly with one seat for each of the seven islands. The council is chaired by the Lantern Regent, who casts the deciding vote whenever the council is tied.

## Origins
Veyra was born from catastrophe. The islands were once the highlands of the old continent of Aumar, which broke apart and sank in an event remembered as the Sundering. Veyrans count their years from the Sundering using the Tideborn Reckoning, abbreviated TR. The present year is 1194 TR.

## The Great Library
The Great Library of Halcyon Reach holds the archives of Veyra. Its keeper is known simply as the Lorekeeper, an archivist famous for answering any question about Veyra, provided the answer has been written down somewhere in the stacks.
