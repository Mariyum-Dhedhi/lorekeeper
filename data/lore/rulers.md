# The Lantern Regents

## The Office of Regent
The Lantern Regent is the head of the Tidecouncil. A Regent is elected by the seven council members and serves for life or until they choose to abdicate. The Regent chairs the council and casts the deciding vote whenever the council is tied. The Regent's symbol of office is a small lantern of driftglass worn on a chain.

## Ysolde Varn (603 to 641 TR)
Ysolde Varn was the first Lantern Regent, chosen at the signing of the Treaty of Saltmere. She reigned from 603 to 641 TR, built the first Tidecouncil chamber in Halcyon Reach, and ordered the construction of the first Lanterns of Calling.

## Corvin Ashe (895 to 914 TR)
Corvin Ashe was Lantern Regent from 895 to 914 TR, and so ruled throughout the Stormwar. Though he could not prevent the war, he negotiated the Treaty of Lanterns that ended it in 911 TR. He abdicated three years later, saying that he had "seen enough storms for one lifetime."

## Maelis Thorne (1179 TR to present)
Maelis Thorne is the current Lantern Regent and has held the office since 1179 TR. She was born on Mirethorn and served as a cartographer for twenty years before joining the Tidecouncil. She is known for carrying a hand-drawn map of the archipelago everywhere she goes and for correcting other people's maps without being asked.
