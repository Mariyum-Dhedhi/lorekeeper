# Tidecraft: The Magic of Veyra

## What Tidecraft Is
Tidecraft is the art of drawing power from the tides of the Glass Sea. A tidecrafter cannot work magic unaided; they must hold a tether stone, a polished focus made from driftglass by the Glasswrights' Guild. Without a tether stone, the tides do not answer.

## The Three Schools
There are three schools of tidecraft.

Currentbinding is the shaping of moving water: calming waves, pulling a ship along a current, or raising a wall of sea. It is the most common school and is taught openly.

Stormweaving is the shaping of weather: summoning wind, rain, and lightning. Stormweaving was banned throughout Veyra by the Treaty of Lanterns in 911 TR, after it caused terrible destruction in the Stormwar. Today only the Tidewardens may practice it, under strict license.

Echosong is the magic of sound and memory. With it, a singer can carry a voice across miles, recall forgotten words, or calm a sky-whale. Echosong is practiced only by the Hollow Choir.

## The Cost of Tidecraft
Every working of tidecraft draws warmth from the body of the crafter. Tidecrafters call this "the chill," and they often wear heavy wool even in summer. A tidecrafter who works too much magic suffers glassing: their skin slowly turns translucent, beginning with the fingertips. Glassing cannot be reversed.
