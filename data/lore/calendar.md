# The Tideborn Calendar

## Structure
The Tideborn Reckoning counts years from the Sundering. A Tideborn year has thirteen months of twenty-eight days each, for a total of 364 days, plus one extra day called Stillday. Stillday belongs to no month and falls between the last day of the old year and the first day of the new year. Each month is divided into four weeks of seven days.

## The Months
The thirteen months, in order, are: Thawtide, Seedtide, Raintide, Bloomtide, Hightide, Suntide, Harvesttide, Emberfall, Mistfall, Leaffall, Frosttide, Deeptide, and Darktide.

## Lantern Night
Lantern Night is celebrated on Stillday. At sunset all forty-nine Lanterns of Calling are extinguished, and at midnight the Lamplighters' Guild relights them one by one, beginning with the Regent's Lantern in Halcyon Reach. Families leave a candle burning in a window until dawn.

## The Festival of Drifting Scales
The Festival of Drifting Scales is held only during a Great Passage, when the sky-whales cross the archipelago. Children collect fallen driftglass, the Hollow Choir sings from every cliff, and work stops for three days.

## Founding Day
Founding Day, on the first day of Hightide, marks the signing of the Treaty of Saltmere and the founding of the Tidecouncil.
